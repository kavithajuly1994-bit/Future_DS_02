📊 Customer Retention & Churn Analysis using Power BI

📌 Project Objective

* Analyze customer churn patterns to identify the key factors influencing customer retention. The dashboard helps answer business questions related to customer behavior, churn drivers, and retention strategies through interactive visualizations.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Excel – Data Cleaning & Validation
* Power BI – Data Visualization & Dashboard Development
* Power Query – Data Transformation
* DAX – KPI Calculations & Measures

📈 KPI Analysis

* 👥 Total Customers
* ❌ Churned Customers
* ✅ Active Customers
* 📉 Churn Rate (%)
* 📈 Retention Rate (%)
* 💰 Total Revenue
* ⏳ Overall Average Tenure (Months)

🎛️ Slicer Implementation

* Contract Type
* Internet Service
* Payment Method
* Gender
* Senior Citizen
* Tech Support

📊 Dashboard Highlights

Dashboard 1 – Customer Churn Overview
* Customer Churn by Contract Type
* Customer Churn by Internet Service Type
* Customer Churn by Payment Method
* Overall Customer Churn Distribution

Dashboard 2 – Customer Segmentation & Retention
* Customer Churn by Tenure Group
* Customer Churn by Monthly Charge Category
* Customer Churn by Tech Support Availability
* Customer Distribution by Gender
* Customer Churn by Senior Citizen Status

💡 Key Insights
* Achieved a 73% customer retention rate with a 27% churn rate.
* Month-to-Month contracts showed the highest customer churn.
* Fiber Optic users and Electronic Check customers had higher churn.
* Customers with shorter tenure and higher monthly charges were more likely to churn.
* Tech Support contributed to improved customer retention.

🎯 Recommendations
* Promote long-term contracts through attractive offers.
* Improve the Fiber Optic service experience.
* Encourage digital payment methods.
* Strengthen first-year customer engagement.
* Increase adoption of Tech Support services.

✅ Conclusion

The dashboard identifies key churn drivers and high-risk customer segments, enabling businesses to implement targeted retention strategies, reduce churn, and improve long-term customer loyalty.
